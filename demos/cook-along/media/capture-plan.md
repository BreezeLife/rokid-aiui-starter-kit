# Cook Along capture plan

This is a plan, not captured product media. Capture owner: hackathon organizer/device tester. Required environment: AIUI Studio and target Rokid glasses on the exact package/runtime recorded by the root verification flow.

Before recording, bind each original file to source fingerprint, AIUI version, device/firmware, `_current` or `_blank`, timestamp and evidence layer. Keep unedited originals and note any crops/captions. Do not present an illustrative image or compatibility-patched browser diagnostic as device evidence.

Recording sequence: open selector; choose Pour-over coffee; Next step to Bloom; Start timer; Pause and Resume; Next step to show timer cancellation and next instruction; move to Serve; Finish; Choose a recipe. Demonstrate the timer's real motion without suggesting a full coffee brew occurred in 30 seconds.

Separate device acceptance captures: touchpad select/activate and focus visibility; root Backspace/exit; return after a retained hidden timer deadline; a new Page after close; bright, dark and cluttered backgrounds; the same essential flow at `_current` size. For the offline check, first open while connected, then disconnect and complete the recipe. Test offline host launch separately and report its actual outcome.

No icon has been created or approved. The owner must supply a custom relevant icon and confirm current portal dimensions/category/media requirements. The official 0.19 [publishing guidance](https://github.com/yodaos-project/AIUI/blob/7d62c2d9ac1cdc1389ba06ed0651b395903accff/documentation/0-guide/bundle/publish.en-US.md) describes 3–5 previews including at least one image and one video; the current authenticated form has not been inspected.
